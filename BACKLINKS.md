# Backlinks & citations — working checklist

Everything here is free and legitimate. Nothing on this list risks a Google
penalty. Work down it in order; the first four are worth more than the rest
of the list put together.

**Do not** buy backlink packages, "5,000 links for $20" gigs, PBNs, or mass
directory blasts. A new domain with one page is exactly the pattern those
get caught on, and digging out of a penalty costs more than the links were
ever worth.

## The block to paste everywhere

Citations work by **agreeing with each other**. Google ties a business
together by seeing the same name, phone and website across the web. One
listing with a different spelling or an old number weakens all of them.

Copy this character for character — same spacing, same punctuation:

```
Luxury Media Wall
+971 56 718 5313
https://luxurymediawall.com/
Dubai, United Arab Emirates
Saturday to Thursday, 9:00 — 19:00
```

**Short description** (fits a 160-character field):

```
Bespoke media walls, TV wall units and custom joinery in Dubai. Marble, fluted timber, backlit onyx and integrated lighting, measured and installed.
```

**Long description** (for fields that allow more):

```
Luxury Media Wall designs, fabricates and installs bespoke media walls and TV
wall units across Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah. Design, fabrication and
installation are handled in house: book-matched marble, fluted timber, backlit
onyx, seamless plaster and split-face stone, with concealed cabling,
ventilation and integrated lighting designed in from the start. Entrance and
hallway joinery, kitchens, wardrobes and full interior renovation.
```

**Categories**, where a site asks for one. Pick in this order of preference:
Interior Designer · Cabinet Maker · Carpenter · Furniture Maker ·
Interior Fitting Contractor

## Tier 1 — this week

### [ ] 1. Google Business Profile  ← the single biggest item

https://business.google.com

No office needed. During setup:

- Answer **no** to "Do you want to add a location customers can visit?"
- Tick **"I deliver goods and services to my customers"**
- Add service areas: **Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah**
- You still give an address for verification (home or workshop). It stays
  hidden from the public listing.
- Verification is usually a video call or a recorded walkthrough showing
  your tools, work and signage. Have those ready.

Afterwards: upload 15–20 project photos, set the website to
`https://luxurymediawall.com/`, and ask three past clients for reviews.
Reviews move the map pack more than almost anything else.

### [ ] 2. Google Search Console

https://search.google.com/search-console

Add the property, verify by DNS (GoDaddy TXT record), then submit
`https://luxurymediawall.com/sitemap.xml`. This is how Google learns the
site moved to the real domain. Without it you are waiting on a crawl that
may take weeks.

### [ ] 3. Bing Places

https://www.bingplaces.com — imports straight from Google Business Profile,
so do it after #1. Ten minutes.

### [ ] 4. Houzz  ← the big one for interiors

https://www.houzz.com — a professional profile, city-level location, no
street address required.

Upload the project photographs with real captions. Houzz is both a strong
link and a genuine source of enquiries for this trade specifically. Worth
an hour of proper effort rather than a rushed profile.

## Tier 3 — the easiest real backlinks you have

These are genuine editorial links and cost nothing but an email.

- [ ] **Stone and surface suppliers** — ask to be listed on their
      "approved installer" or "where to buy" page. You are already a
      customer; most say yes.
- [ ] **LED and lighting suppliers** — same ask.
- [ ] **Veneer and board suppliers** — same ask.
- [ ] **Appliance brands** whose units you recess — several run installer
      directories.
- [ ] **Contractors and developers** you have worked with — subcontractor
      or partner pages.
- [ ] **Interior designers** who specify your joinery — "trusted trades"
      pages.

A template that works better than a generic request:

> Hi [name],
>
> We fit [their product] into bespoke media walls across Dubai — the last
> one was [brief, specific detail]. If you keep a list of installers or
> partners on your site, we would be glad to be on it.
>
> Luxury Media Wall — https://luxurymediawall.com/
>
> Happy to send photographs of the installs if useful.

## Tier 4 — earning links

The site is one page. There is nothing to link to except the homepage,
which limits what anyone *can* link to.

You have real expertise that nobody writes up well:

- TV height against seating distance, and why most walls get it wrong
- Cable routing and heat in a sealed recess
- What a Dubai apartment fit-out actually involves: AC points, socket
  positions, lift protection, community working hours, parking
- Marble vs porcelain vs microcement for a feature wall, honestly compared

A proper guide on any of these earns links on its own, and ranks for
searches that lead to enquiries. **Ask me to build these pages** — they
are the highest-return thing left on the site.

## What not to bother with

- Generic "submit your site to 500 directories" services
- Article spinning / mass guest posting
- Link exchanges at scale
- Comment links on blogs and forums

None of these move a local trade business, and several carry risk.
